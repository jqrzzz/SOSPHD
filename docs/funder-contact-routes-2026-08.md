# Funder contact routes — verified 2026-08-15

Produced by a 10-agent search + adversarial-verification workflow over the eleven
company-eligible funding opportunities. **15 routes found → 14 survived, 1 rejected.**

## The finding that matters most

**US federal SBIR/STTR may be structurally closed to this project, not merely difficult.**
Verification established three published rules that compound:

1. R&D must be performed **in its entirety in the United States**.
2. **NIH no longer recognises foreign subawards** — a Thai partner site cannot be funded as a
   subaward at all. Only foreign consultants, foreign vendors, or non-monetary collaboration.
3. A foreign-performance waiver **cannot be pre-approved** — it is considered only once an
   application is already being considered for award.

Together these mean the Thailand fieldwork is the obstacle, and incorporating a US entity does
not by itself solve it. That reorders the 30 November "incorporate a US entity" decision:
**make the programme-officer call first**, as a go/no-go sounding, before spending money.

The verifier also corrected a first-pass error worth recording: it had proposed asking NIH
whether a Thai university could hold the STTR partner role. That is published and the answer is
no — the partner must be a US-located non-profit. Asking it would have signalled the programme
rules had not been read.

## Reauthorisation context

SBIR/STTR authority lapsed 1 October 2025 and all open NOFOs were closed. It was reauthorised
through FY2031 by Public Law 119-83, signed 13 April 2026. The programmes are live again.
