# Paper 1 journal shortlist — 2026-08-31

Official-publisher comparison for the current approximately 11,000-word Paper 1.
Fees and requirements must be rechecked on the actual submission date.

No manuscript was submitted.

## Ranking

1. **BMC Health Services Research** — best overall scope fit.
2. **Frontiers in Health Services, Health Policy and Management** — best
   current-format fit.
3. **PLOS ONE** — broad soundness-based fallback.
4. **Journal of Travel Medicine** — topical stretch requiring a separate short
   manuscript.

## 1. BMC Health Services Research — recommended first submission

### Why it fits

The journal directly covers healthcare delivery, access, quality, safety,
governance, management, and digital-health measurement. Paper 1 should be framed
as a health-system measurement failure with transferable implications—not an
audit of one company.

The journal cautions that single-region descriptive work needs genuinely new,
internationally relevant insight. It also excludes work oriented toward
monetising health data or increasing for-profit revenue. The manuscript must
therefore state the non-commercial research purpose, Tourist SOS ownership,
conflict management, and why the measurement failure matters beyond one service.

- [Official aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/12913/aims-and-scope)

### Format

- Article type: Research Article.
- Structured abstract: maximum 350 words.
- No published main-text word ceiling.
- No stated combined table/figure ceiling.
- Figures upload separately; large tables and ancillary methods/data can be
  supplementary files.
- A data-availability statement is mandatory; restricted company-held data can
  be described with access conditions.
- Use STROBE and RECORD checklists as supplementary material.

The current 11,000-word draft is technically possible, but should still be
tightened for clarity rather than left long merely because no hard limit exists.

- [Research Article requirements](https://link.springer.com/journal/12913/submission-guidelines/research-article)

### Cost and submission

- Fully open access.
- Current APC listed as £2,590 / US$3,450 / €3,050 plus applicable tax.
- Country-tiering and waivers may apply and normally need to be requested during
  submission.

- [Official publication fee page](https://link.springer.com/journal/12913/how-to-publish-with-us)
- [Submission portal](https://submission.springernature.com/new-submission/12913/3)

### Main rejection risk

Insufficient international/system-level generalization or an unclear boundary
between research and the commercial operator.

## 2. Frontiers in Health Services — easiest current-format fallback

### Why it fits

The Health Policy and Management section covers healthcare design and delivery,
information systems, patient safety, health-system efficiency, and management.
It rejects descriptions of a single service without a broader system-level
perspective. The manuscript must centre the multi-actor tourist-care corridor
and transferable measurement problem.

- [Journal scope](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/about)
- [Health Policy and Management section](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/sections/health-policy-and-management)

### Format

- Article type: Original Research.
- Main text: maximum 12,000 words.
- Abstract: maximum 350 words.
- Maximum 15 figures/tables combined.
- Supplementary files: up to 30 MB each; archived in Figshare and not typeset.
- STROBE and RECORD are explicitly relevant; upload completed checklists.

The current manuscript fits the word limit with little structural compression.

- [Article-type limits](https://www.frontiersin.org/for-authors/where-to-publish/article-types)
- [Author guidelines](https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/author-guidelines)
- [Reporting policy](https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/policies-and-publication-ethics)

### Cost and submission

- Gold open access, CC BY.
- Current Original Research APC: CHF 3,150.
- Institutional support or fee assistance may apply.

- [Official fees](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/for-authors/publishing-fees)
- [Submission portal](https://www.frontiersin.org/submission/submit?domainid=2&entityid=2067&entitytype=2&fieldid=172&specialtyid=2497)

### Main rejection risk

The paper reads as a company dataset description rather than a general
health-services measurement contribution.

## 3. PLOS ONE — validity-based fallback

### Why it fits

PLOS ONE evaluates scientific validity, methods, reporting, and ethics rather
than perceived impact. A well-supported negative finding about missing routine
coordination data fits that model, although the readership is less concentrated
in health services and travel medicine.

- [Scope and publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/journal-information)

### Format

- Article type: Research Article.
- No manuscript word, figure, or supporting-information limit.
- Abstract: maximum 300 words.
- Figures upload separately; tables remain in the manuscript.
- STROBE is required for observational work; add RECORD for routinely collected
  data.
- PLOS expects access to the data needed to reproduce conclusions. The ethics
  and legal basis for restricting company-held case data must be settled before
  submission.

- [Submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines)
- [Supporting information](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/supporting-information)

### Cost and submission

- Fully open access, CC BY.
- Current “all other articles” fee: US$2,382, at the rate effective on
  submission.
- Institutional coverage and fee assistance may be available; assistance is
  requested during submission.

- [Official PLOS fees](https://plos.org/publish/fees/)
- [Submit](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submit-now)

### Main rejection risk

Restricted data availability or insufficiently explicit ethics/waiver grounds.

## 4. Journal of Travel Medicine — separate short-paper option

### Why it fits

This has the most direct audience for international travellers, travel-related
emergencies, surveillance, and travel-health policy. The cohort is topical, but
the manuscript's health-system measurement contribution is not the journal's
most natural format.

- [Official scope](https://academic.oup.com/jtm/pages/about)

### Format

- Article type: Original Article.
- Main text: 2,500–3,800 words.
- Structured abstract: maximum 300 words.
- References: up to 70.
- Tables/figures: maximum 4–5 combined.
- STROBE applies; RECORD should be added.

The current manuscript would need a deliberate reduction of roughly two thirds.
Core methods and results cannot simply be hidden in supplements.

- [Author instructions](https://academic.oup.com/jtm/pages/general_instructions)

### Cost and submission

- Hybrid journal; standard publication licence has no page charges.
- Optional immediate-open-access pricing is provided through OUP's current
  calculator rather than a stable amount on the journal page.

- [ScholarOne submission](https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jtm)

### Main rejection risk

The health-systems argument is too long and too indirect for the travel-medicine
article format.

## Recommended submission strategy

1. Prepare the manuscript and cover letter for **BMC Health Services Research**.
2. Keep **Frontiers in Health Services** as the low-reformat fallback.
3. Keep **PLOS ONE** as the broad validity-based fallback.
4. Do not compress the current paper for Journal of Travel Medicine unless the
   first three routes fail or a separate traveller-focused short paper is
   strategically valuable.

## Human gates

- confirm whether the open-access cost is acceptable or waiver-eligible;
- approve the conflict-of-interest and company-data framing;
- approve the target journal before the manuscript is reformatted;
- confirm the ethics/waiver and restricted-data statement.
