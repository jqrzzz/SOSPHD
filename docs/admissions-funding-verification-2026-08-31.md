## Admissions decisions

### 1. Mahidol Public Health Administration PhD — active, conditional

The relevant research degree is Mahidol's **Doctor of Philosophy in Public
Health Administration (Special Program)**. It must not be conflated with the
Faculty's English-language DrPH.

It is also not the same programme as the current live-database Mahidol row,
which names the Faculty of Tropical Medicine's international PhD. Do not
overwrite that row with these facts. If the Thai-language gate is acceptable,
the Public Health Administration programme needs its own programme record; the
Tropical Medicine route still requires a separate official verification pass.

Verified position:

- The special PhD has weekend/public-holiday delivery; the current class
  schedule includes Saturday/Sunday and online sessions.
- The official curriculum states that the programme language is Thai. It is not
  an English-language or distance PhD.
- Entry normally requires a relevant Master's degree, GPA at least 3.50, and a
  Mahidol doctoral English score. No GRE requirement is listed.
- No published rule requires supervisor consent before applying; selection is
  document review plus interview.
- The programme is being advertised for AY2026 semester two, but the exact
  closing date for programme `2343DS00` was not established. The AY2027 window
  is unknown.
- The official curriculum estimates THB 555,000 over the three-year plan.
- Mahidol's 2026 international-entry scholarship round is complete; applicability
  to this Thai special programme and the 2027 route remain unknown.

Decision: **active only if Thai-language study and defence are acceptable and
the department confirms an actionable application window.** Bangkok location
alone is not sufficient fit.

### 2. NUS Saw Swee Hock PhD — active backup

Verified position:

- The August 2027 intake closes **1 November 2026**.
- Applicants must contact a prospective faculty thesis advisor and jointly
  identify the project before applying.
- Relevant Master's, good second-upper-equivalent Bachelor's, MBBS, or BDS routes
  are listed.
- GRE is not mandatory, although NUS says it is strongly encouraged.
- IELTS Academic 7.0 or the published TOEFL threshold applies unless an eligible
  English-medium degree waiver applies; US English-medium degrees are included.

operationally compatible until residency, immigration, and continued-company-work

### 1. Laerdal Foundation Project Support — active preparation, eligibility query first

Verified position:

- The application platform was open on **5 September 2026** and lists the
  **1 October 2026** autumn deadline. No official cutoff time or timezone was
  found.
- Project support is up to USD 50,000 for a 12–18 month project.
- Supported work needs a research dimension and must address sudden cardiac
  arrest, trauma, or birth. Trauma, emergency care, and time-critical conditions
  are named priorities.
- Commercial product development, equipment-only requests, construction, and
  conference/travel-led requests are normally excluded.
- The official pages do not establish that an unaffiliated individual or Tourist
  SOS as a Thai company is eligible. The old sweep must not present either route
  as verified.

Decision: **prepare a non-commercial prospective trauma/emergency-care concept,
but ask the narrow applicant-eligibility question before substantial drafting.**

### 3. NUS Research Scholarship — active only with the NUS application

Verified position:

- The scholarship is requested inside the NUS admission application; there is
  no separate scholarship form for this route.
- It is open to international research students and provides tuition support and
  a monthly stipend, normally for up to four years for a PhD.
- International recipients participate in the Graduate Assistantship Programme.
- Employment, another scholarship, fellowship, or allowance requires prior NUS
  approval.

Decision: **treat this as part of the NUS application, not as a separate grant
pipeline.** Advisor fit and operational compatibility come first.
